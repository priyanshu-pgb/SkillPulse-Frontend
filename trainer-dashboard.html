The 4 Triangulation Vectors: How Outcomes Are Discovered Without Phone Access

Trainees from remote geographies or seasonal migration corridors cannot be tracked through standard calls. Click any of the 4 vectors below to trigger automated institutional discovery:

PILLAR 1 · STATUTORY AUTOMATION

EPFO / ESIC Provident Fund Ping

"The Boss & Bank Footprint": Every registered enterprise in India must deposit monthly PF for workers. We query the EPFO gateway using tokenized Aadhaar/UAN to find employment status and employer name without calling the trainee.

PILLAR 2 · LAST-MILE HUMAN MESH

Gram Panchayat & ASHA Doorstep

"The Family & Neighbor Check": Trainee moved to a metro, but parents still live in the village. Panchayat Sahayaks & ASHA Didis visit households weekly and record 1-tap doorstep confirmation of migration city and role.

PILLAR 3 · REVERSE RECRUITER MATCH

Rozgar Mela Recruiter Roster Sync

"The Hiring List": Cross-references unreachable candidate IDs against bulk candidate placement rosters uploaded by corporate recruiters (auto-clusters, textile mills, logistics) and staffing agencies (TeamLease/Quess).

PILLAR 4 · FINANCIAL INCENTIVE RAIL

₹1,500 PPS Direct Benefit & Missed Call

"The Incentive Carrot": Under DDU-GKY, rural migrants receive ₹1,500/mo allowance for 3 months. Trainees simply give a free missed call to 1800-889-PULSE to claim rent money and confirm their work location.
